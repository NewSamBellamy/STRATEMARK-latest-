# Sprint — 2026-07-30 · "The second brain"

One sprint, built from the founder's recorded audit (the two Ivory Goose videos + written
walkthrough). Everything below is implemented, tested, and verified against rendered pixels.

## The conversation layer (the centerpiece)

**Every data point can start a grounded conversation, and every conversation becomes part of
the deck.** This is the second-brain loop: two analysts researching the same market end up with
different decks because their questions differ.

- **Dig** — the shovel. Icon-only affordance beside every figure, metric tile, timeline
  milestone, product, org-chart person, intel story, and market card. Opens the right-hand
  sheet as a multi-turn conversation seeded with that data point.
- **Grounding contract** (`CHAT_SYSTEM`): answers come ONLY from (1) the deck's stored research
  — confidence tags respected and repeated — plus (2) a fresh Google Search. Never training
  data. If neither supports a claim, the copilot says so.
- **Threads persist** on the snapshot (`RepoSnapshot.threads`), listed newest-first in the
  **Research** history dropdown on both the deck page and every company dashboard. Reopen any
  thread with full history.
- **Save as report**: any thread distills into a saved report (optional focus prompt). The
  report lands in the Reports library, the company's intel file, and the thread links to it.
- **Deck-level chat**: "Ask deck" opens a conversation over the whole deck. **Compare** enters
  selection mode — click cards to select, then "Ask about these" scopes the conversation to
  exactly those cards.
- **Custom report focus**: the Report buttons (deck + company) take an optional "what should
  this concentrate on" prompt. Focus steers emphasis; sourcing rules don't change.
- Transports without conversations (the Electron IPC bridge, until the desktop back end wires
  them) fall back to the one-shot deep dive automatically — feature-detected, nothing breaks.

## Dashboard upgrades (from the audit, tab by tab)

- **All 8 tabs prefetch** the moment the dashboard opens — sequential, rate-limit-friendly, so
  by the time you finish the overview the rest are warm.
- **Metrics**: KPI band up top (all five headline figures + confidence dots), then the tile
  grid (share donut, band gauges, fact-check, correct-this-figure, shovel), then trend charts.
- **History**: the company story as a readable one-pager, then the timeline as a rail of dated
  cards (quarterly for young companies, yearly for older — the research prompt decides from
  the sources), each milestone diggable. Quotes keep their rail.
- **Products & Roadmap**: products ranked breadwinners-first with an honest `revenueNote`
  ("~78% of FY25 revenue per 10-K" / "not disclosed" — never an invented figure), shovel per
  product, roadmap in Now/Next/Later lanes.
- **Team & Org**: hover a person for their sourced 1-2 sentence bio; click to research them
  (opens a conversation scoped to that person).
- **Live Intel**: no dead links (headlines only link when a real URL exists), publisher named
  on every link, shovel per story ("Dig into this story").
- **Live Landing**: "Audit this page" — a grounded landing-page audit as a conversation.
- **Overview**: now researches "Who they sell to" (target customer) as its own section.

## Card & deck fixes (straight from the videos)

- **Card reader is wide and scroll-free**: three panes — the card · key metrics with evidence ·
  CMS breakdown side by side.
- **Back to deck actually goes back to the deck** (`?deck=` param carried through dashboard
  tabs; history fallback only without deck context).
- **One-liner clip fixed** (line-clamp now real), **stat bars slimmed**.
- **Insight cards open into a reader**: title, one-liner, numbered key points (researched in
  the same grounded pass — zero extra quota), source publisher, Dig.
- **Repeatable generative artwork** for Insight / Barrier / Vice / Culture cards — deterministic
  SVG motifs seeded by title (arcs / columns / hazard weave / community dots). Free, keyless,
  stable across re-renders, works for any market.
- **Vice claims now record their publisher** (`sourceTitle`) at research time. The shipped
  snapshot recovered 2/21 titles by URL-matching stored citations; the rest honestly show
  "Publisher not recorded" — future keyed runs record all of them.

## Pipeline & trust (measured on the shipped bake)

- One entity card per company (was: one per role — the same figures printed three times).
  Signal cards mint only when they carry content (sourced claim / culture note).
- Discovery assigns ONE primary role with real criteria + signals only when reported.
- Tolerant dashboard schemas: a missing sub-field degrades the tab, never blanks it
  (was 21% tab loss on a live bake; this bake: **0 failures, 80/80 tabs**).
- Baked sample deck (Frontier AI Ecosystem, 10 companies, 26 cards, 150 API calls):
  **all 7 card types present · 25/25 verified figures carry citations · 0 violations ·
  report + whitespace analysis included.**
- Bake script writes through to disk on every mutation (crash-safe, resumable via `MI_TOPUP=1`).
