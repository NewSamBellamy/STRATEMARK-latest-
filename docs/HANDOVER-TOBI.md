## Phase 2 — the design pass (after Maruf's flag)

**Scope: visual polish only.** No feature changes, no flow changes — flows were audited across 30+ founder review rounds and every button works. If a flow feels wrong, flag it to Shannon; don't redesign it silently.

### Where the design system lives

- Tokens: `apps/web/tailwind.config.*` + `apps/web/src/index.css` (semantic classes: `panel`, `chip`, `btn-primary`, `btn-ghost`, `input`, `label`; color roles: `surface`, `surface-2`, `content`, `muted`, `faint`, `border`, `primary`, `positive`, `negative`).
- Both light and dark themes ship — check every change in both.
- Type: `font-display` for headings/numbers, body default elsewhere.

### The surfaces, in judging order (what the hackathon video will show)

1. **New Deck** (`features/deck/NewDeckPage.tsx`) — the conversational research flow + live progress
2. **Deck view** (`features/deck/DeckPage.tsx`, `features/card/GameCard.tsx`) — the cards themselves; tier styling is intentionally subtle (founder-tuned twice — don't re-loud it)
3. **All decks shelf** (`features/markets/MarketsListPage.tsx`) — carousel of card stacks with generated face art
4. **Company dashboard** (`features/dashboard/*`) — 8 tabs; Metrics and Team & Org are the showpieces
5. **Daily Briefing** (`features/briefing/*`) — the unboxing reveal is the product's signature moment; polish, don't restructure
6. **Reports** (`features/reports/*`) — editorial masthead style, PDF/pptx/share
7. **Share landing** (`features/share/SharePage.tsx`) — what recipients see; it should look like a gift
8. **Settings** (`features/settings/SettingsPage.tsx`) — key, usage & billing, cost controls, pricing

### Hard rules carried over from founder rounds

- **No sparkle icons anywhere.** AI-generated images carry the quiet italic caption instead ("This image was generated by AI") — keep it.
- Honest states are sacred: "Unknown" slots, anti-bot fallbacks, elapsed-time loaders — restyle them, never hide them.
- The 2K-style rating (never a perfect 100) and confidence badges are product identity, not decoration.
- Every image the app paid for persists; don't introduce designs that require regenerating art.

### Assets you'll want for the landing page

- Screenshots after your pass (light + dark), the unboxing animation as video, a deck-shelf hero shot, and the architecture diagram from `README.md` (judges + buyers both like it).
